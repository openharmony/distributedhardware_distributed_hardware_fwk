## Summary

Adds mutex protection to ComponentLoader::IsDHTypeExist, IsDHTypeSinkLoaded, IsDHTypeSourceLoaded, IsDHTypeHandlerLoaded. Previously these methods accessed compHandlerMap_ without holding compHandlerMapMutex_, creating a data race. The fix uses find() instead of operator[] (eliminating the side effect of inserting default entries) and copies handler pointers under lock before checking outside the lock. No defects found.
